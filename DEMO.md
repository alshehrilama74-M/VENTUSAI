# VENTUSAI dashboard walkthrough

Open: https://alshehrilama74-M.github.io/VENTUSAI/

1. **Overview**: explain 10 turbines, cleaned records, 48 sensor features, and the two-stage RF → SVM system.
2. **Diagnostic Simulator**: choose Normal Operation and then Lubrication Fault; move one temperature slider and observe the illustrative response.
3. **Fleet Monitor**: click a turbine to inspect simulated telemetry.
4. **Model Benchmarks**: compare Stage 1 and Stage 2 validation metrics and the separately reported held-out test scores.

**Disclosure**: The simulator is rule-based and illustrates the diagnostic workflow; it does **not** provide live inference from the saved machine-learning model. The benchmark numbers are based on offline model evaluation.

Academic presentation: 15 minutes + 5 minutes for Q&A.
